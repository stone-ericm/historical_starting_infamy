# Not representable under v1

Candidate acts the ledger cannot express under the spec §5 rules, with the rule that excludes each. These are omissions from the derivation, stated so the README's claim is honest.

- **US Indian Removal 1830–36 and the Black Hawk War 1832** — the lands taken were parts of 1836 USA slices (Georgia, Alabama, Mississippi, Illinois); whole-slice-only rule.
- **Coorg (1834) and Cachar (1832) annexations by the East India Company** — parts of 1836 BIC slices (Mysore, Assam); whole-slice-only rule. Note (2026-10-14): the 1824 Burmese-war Assam goal (`bic_assam`) prices the whole BIC Assam slice, Cachar included; that over-coverage is declared on the component rather than refused (owner choice under the v4 'defensible estimate' ruling; BIC's balance decays to zero in every table either way).
- **Ottoman reassertion over Tripoli, 1835** — Tripoli is already an Ottoman puppet in vanilla 1836 and stays one; the only matching analog is `reduce_autonomy`, which the spec excludes.
- **Caucasian War campaigns 1817–1835** — no 1836 slice changed hands inside the window; Russia's Dagestan and Kuban slices date from 1783–1813.
- **Austrian interventions in the Papal States 1831–32; French occupation of Ancona 1832** — restorations and garrisons with no territorial or subjugation demand; no infamy-generating analog.
- **Zulu campaigns under Dingane 1828–35** — no change of a 1836 slice; the Zulu kingdom's only state is its home state.
- **Russian protectorate over Moldavia and Wallachia (Adrianople, 1829)** — both remain Ottoman subjects in vanilla 1836; no subject transfer to represent.
- **Egyptian administration of Crete from 1830** — granted by the Sultan, not conquered.
- **Spanish Haiti's own secession from Spain (December 1821)** — independence generates no infamy.
