# Sources

One block per `source` key in `acts.csv`. Each gives the event, the dates the ledger uses, and a general reference a reader can check. Where sources disagree the ledger's choice is stated.

- **kutahya** — Egyptian–Ottoman War (1831–1833) and the Convention of Kütahya. Ibrahim Pasha entered Palestine late October 1831 and besieged Acre from November 1831; the convention of May 1833 left Syria, Palestine and Adana to Muhammad Ali. Wikipedia: "Egyptian–Ottoman War (1831–1833)", "Convention of Kütahya".
- **turco_egyptian_sudan** — Egyptian conquest of the Sudan 1820–21. Ismail Pasha's force left in October 1820; Dongola submitted by November 1820; Sennar fell in June 1821 (end of the Funj sultanate); Kordofan after the battle of Bara, August 1821. Wikipedia: "Turco-Egyptian Sudan"; BnF authority record dating the fall of Sennar to June 1821; Lutsky, *Modern History of the Arab Countries* (1969), ch. 7.
- **peru_bolivian_confederation** — Santa Cruz's intervention from June 1835; Confederation proclaimed 28 October 1836. Wikipedia: "Peru–Bolivian Confederation". Computed but never emitted: vanilla gives Bolivia 50.
- **turkmenchay** — Russo-Persian War (1826–1828): Persian attack July 1826; Treaty of Turkmenchay 22 February 1828 ceded the Erivan and Nakhchivan khanates. Wikipedia: "Russo-Persian War (1826–1828)", "Treaty of Turkmenchay".
- **adrianople** — Russo-Turkish War (1828–1829): declared 26 April 1828; Treaty of Adrianople 14 September 1829 gave Russia the Danube delta. Wikipedia: "Russo-Turkish War (1828–1829)", "Treaty of Adrianople (1829)".
- **yandabo** — First Anglo-Burmese War, declared 5 March 1824; Treaty of Yandabo 24 February 1826 ceded Arakan, Tenasserim, Assam and Manipur. Wikipedia: "First Anglo-Burmese War", "Treaty of Yandabo".
- **greek_independence** — Treaty of London 6 July 1827 (Britain, France, Russia); London Protocol 3 February 1830 recognising Greek independence. Wikipedia: "Treaty of London (1827)", "London Protocol (1830)".
- **algiers_1830** — French landing at Sidi Ferruch 14 June 1830; Algiers 5 July 1830; Oran occupied 4 January 1831; Bône 27 March 1832. Wikipedia: "Invasion of Algiers in 1830", "French conquest of Algeria".
- **belgian_revolution** — London Protocol of 20 December 1830 recognising Belgian independence; Treaty of London 19 April 1839 (Dutch acceptance). Wikipedia: "Belgian Revolution", "Treaty of London (1839)". Pending on 1836-01-01.
- **ten_days_campaign** — 2–12 August 1831; Dutch withdrawal under French intervention. Wikipedia: "Ten Days' Campaign".
- **peshawar_1834** — Capture of Peshawar by Hari Singh Nalwa; formal annexation 6 May 1834. Wikipedia: "Capture of Peshawar (1834)".
- **ladakh_1834** — Dogra invasion of Ladakh, August 1834 to October 1835, led by Zorawar Singh under Gulab Singh, Ranjit Singh's vassal. Wikipedia: "Dogra invasion of Ladakh". Pending because Ladakh is independent in vanilla 1836.
- **tay_thanh** — Siamese–Vietnamese War 23 November 1833 – April 1834; Cambodia placed under direct Vietnamese rule as Tây Thành in late 1834. Wikipedia: "Siamese–Vietnamese War (1833–1834)", "Tây Thành province", "Vietnamese invasions of Cambodia".
- **siam_vietnam_1833** — Same war from the Siamese side; Bodindecha's force withdrew by April 1834. Wikipedia: "Siamese–Vietnamese War (1833–1834)".
- **lao_rebellion** — Anouvong's advance January 1827; Vientiane taken in 1827 and destroyed in 1828; the kingdom annexed. Wikipedia: "Lao rebellion (1826–1828)"; Britannica, "Chao Anu". Sources differ between May 1827 and 1828 for the final destruction; the ledger enforces at 31 October 1828.
- **kedah_1821** — Siamese invasion of Kedah, 12 November 1821; Kedah fort taken 18 November 1821. Wikipedia: "Siamese invasion of Kedah"; Sharom Ahmat, "Kedah–Siam relations 1821–1905", *Journal of the Siam Society* 59 (1971).
- **hispaniola_1822** — Boyer's army entered Santo Domingo on 9 February 1822. Wikipedia: "Unification of Hispaniola".
- **barradas** — Spanish landing at Tampico 27 July 1829; capitulation 11 September 1829. Wikipedia: "Spanish attempts to reconquer Mexico", "Battle of Tampico (1829)".
- **herat_1833** — Siege of Herat, summer to November 1833; lifted after Abbas Mirza's death with only nominal suzerainty acknowledged. Wikipedia: "Siege of Herat (1833)", "Herat (1793–1863)".
- **radama_i** — Radama I's conquests: east coast 1817, the west reduced by 1824. Wikipedia: "Radama I"; Library of Congress country study, "Madagascar: Historical Setting".
